# Scoop promo video: Higgsfield prompts

**Story:** Two friends want something fun to do this week. They ask Scoop in plain English, then pick their
Creative vibe, and Scoop ranks the best match: a free **Lunchtime Gallery Talk** (97% match). They book it,
Scoop shows the MRT route and taxi fare, and they enjoy the talk.

## Settings for every clip

- Model: **Seedance 2.5**
- Aspect ratio: **9:16**
- Resolution: **1080p**
- Clips with a screenshot: upload it as the **start frame / reference image** (Clip 4 also uses an **end frame**)

**Style line: paste it at the end of every prompt:**

```
Bright upbeat lifestyle ad, warm Bangkok daylight, fresh green accent color, two Thai friends in their 20s, smooth handheld camera, tack sharp, vertical social video.
```
